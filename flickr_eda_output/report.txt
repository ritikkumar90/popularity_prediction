======== 11. DESIGN NOTES (rule-based; read together with the numbers above) ========
1. Target (30d markings): median 1, skew 19.4 (log1p skew 1.6), 19% zeros. Use log1p regression, or a classification/hurdle formulation if zeros dominate.
2. Smallest horizon capturing >=80% of a photo's observed markings: 90 days. A horizon below that measures a growth stage, not final popularity - decide which one you want to predict.
3. Early markings predict REMAINING markings with Spearman 0.03 at best (window 1h); 77.8% of photos have no early marking at that window. Early counts are weak: lean on owner/social features and expect a low ceiling.
4. Ignore 'vs total' correlations; they are mechanical.
5. Never use views / comments / favorites columns or total-uploads-per-owner as features: they are crawl-time or future information (see LEAK rows in section 9).
6. Target distribution shifts over time (KS D=0.20; median 1 -> 1): use a temporal split and consider recency-normalised targets.
7. Cold start: 22% of test fans and 36% of test owners are unseen in train: avoid pure ID embeddings; prefer degree/activity features and neighbour aggregation (inductive).
8. 51% of links share one timestamp (first-crawl snapshot): treat the social graph as static.
9. In-degree is extremely skewed: log-transform degree features, cap neighbour fan-outs, use neighbour sampling.
10. Social channel: 51% of sampled markings come from fans following the owner, 30% from fans following an earlier fan, 38% have no social path (discovery by search/Explore). Descriptive only; compute any exposure feature inside the early window.
11. ~125,320,105 edges in the full heterograph: use mini-batch neighbour sampling around target photos (NeighborLoader/HGTLoader); full-batch R-GCN/HGT will not fit.
12. Message passing must only use markings up to the prediction time: that keeps 38.6% of favorite edges. Build one graph snapshot per observation window.
13. No node attributes exist: initialise Users with log-degree/activity features and Photos with early-window statistics + owner features; pure ID embeddings generalise poorly to new photos.
14. XGBoost baseline: use photo_table_sample.csv (without LEAK columns); every graph model must beat it on the temporal test split.
