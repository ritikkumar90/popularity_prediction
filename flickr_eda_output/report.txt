======== 11. DESIGN NOTES (rule-based; read together with the numbers above) ========
1. Target (30d markings): median 58, skew 15.1 (log1p skew 1.6), 0% zeros. Use log1p regression, or a classification/hurdle formulation if zeros dominate.
2. Smallest horizon capturing >=80% of a photo's observed markings: 90 days. A horizon below that measures a growth stage, not final popularity - decide which one you want to predict.
3. Early markings predict REMAINING markings with Spearman 0.41 at best (window 168h); 0.2% of photos have no early marking at that window. Early counts are informative: they should be the core XGBoost feature.
4. Ignore 'vs total' correlations; they are mechanical.
5. Never use views / comments / favorites columns or total-uploads-per-owner as features: they are crawl-time or future information (see LEAK rows in section 9).
6. Target distribution shifts over time (KS D=0.27; median 56 -> 65): use a temporal split and consider recency-normalised targets.
7. Cold start: 27% of test fans and 96% of test owners are unseen in train: avoid pure ID embeddings; prefer degree/activity features and neighbour aggregation (inductive).
8. 49% of links share one timestamp (first-crawl snapshot): treat the social graph as static.
9. In-degree is extremely skewed: log-transform degree features, cap neighbour fan-outs, use neighbour sampling.
10. Social channel: 2% of sampled markings come from fans following the owner, 42% from fans following an earlier fan, 57% have no social path (discovery by search/Explore). Descriptive only; compute any exposure feature inside the early window.
11. Message passing must only use markings up to the prediction time: that keeps 7.5% of favorite edges. Build one graph snapshot per observation window.
12. No node attributes exist: initialise Users with log-degree/activity features and Photos with early-window statistics + owner features; pure ID embeddings generalise poorly to new photos.
13. XGBoost baseline: use photo_table_sample.csv (without LEAK columns); every graph model must beat it on the temporal test split.
